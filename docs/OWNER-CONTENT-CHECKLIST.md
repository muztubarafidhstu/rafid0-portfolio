# What to prepare for your website (rafid0.com)

You add all of this yourself in the Admin Panel (https://rafid0.com/admin) after the site is live.
Collect it now, so going live is quick.

## Profile
- [ ] Profile photo: square or portrait, clear face, JPG/PNG
- [ ] Short intro (1–2 sentences)
- [ ] Full biography (1–3 short paragraphs)
- [ ] **CV as PDF** (this is what the "Download CV" button downloads)
- [ ] Experience: for each job: years, job title, company, one-line description
- [ ] Education: years, degree, university
- [ ] Skills: grouped, e.g. "Embedded: ESP32, STM32, FreeRTOS"
- [ ] Awards & achievements: year, title, details

## Home page
- [ ] A wide photo of your best work for the top of the home page (robot, hardware, lab)
- [ ] Your real numbers: projects completed, years of experience, papers, awards…

## Projects (for each one)
- [ ] Title, one-line description, category, technologies
- [ ] 3–10 good photos (the first one is the cover)
- [ ] System block diagram (image), if you have one
- [ ] Key features, specifications, results (numbers)
- [ ] Video: YouTube link (best) or a short MP4
- [ ] Documents: PDF report, manual, BOM, source code ZIP…

## Research
- [ ] For each paper or patent: title, journal or patent office, year, authors, abstract, link or DOI, PDF

## Gallery & Downloads
- [ ] Extra photos: lab, field work, events, awards
- [ ] Files to share: datasets, code, documents (PDF/ZIP)

## Contact & social
- [ ] Email (ideally me@rafid0.com, forwarded to your Gmail)
- [ ] Phone (optional), city
- [ ] LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube, Google Scholar, ResearchGate links. Use **rafid0** as your username everywhere

## Before going live, replace the sample content
- [ ] Sample pictures on the 7 projects
- [ ] Sample job history, education and awards (they are examples, not real)
